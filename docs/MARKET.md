# STARLEAP — market read for a $0.99 App Store release (2026-10-11)

Evidence is thin and partly dated; treat as hypotheses to test, not conclusions.

## Competitive set (App Store, from listing pages)
| App | Price | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese Checkers Online | Free + IAP, "no ads" | 4.5★ / 876 ratings — online matchmaking, friend games, bot levels |
| Chinese Checkers – Jump Game | Free + IAP $1.99–$7.99 | ~4.45★ / ~2K (aggregator, low confidence) |
| Chinese Checkers HD | Free | Claims 5M+ downloads (self-reported, old) |
| Chinese Checkers Master | $5.00 + IAP | 3.1★ / 27 — a paid one is barely rated |
| Chinese Checkers Touch | Free | 1.7★ / 63 |
| Halma – brain game, others | Free | — |

## What this implies
- The category is crowded with **free** apps; the only well-rated one has online play. A bare $0.99
  Chinese Checkers loses to free on price and to Online on multiplayer.
- Paid-up-front is a niche model; indie premium works mainly via editorial features (Apple Indie
  Games page) and distinctive design (Monument Valley, $3.99). Plan for a featuring pitch, not
  organic search.
- STARLEAP's real differentiators are rules and content, not "Chinese Checkers": chained long-jumps,
  no blocking stalemate, a named AI ladder verified by self-play, and (new) proven-optimal puzzles.
  The puzzle mode is the strongest hook — there is no competitor with solver-verified puzzles.
- $0.99 is a low-value price point: Apple's cut + refunds + support cost leave little; revenue at
  realistic volumes is small. The product's value is more likely in brand credibility (Inventor's
  first app) than in income. Alternative: free with a one-time "Unlock all puzzles" IAP.

## Cheapest ways to test demand before building the store version
1. Family/friends play on the live Pages site; watch for voluntary retries and shares.
2. Publish the puzzle mode as a free web page with a share link and waitlist; measure completions.
3. Free TestFlight beta (needs the Capacitor wrapper) with 20–50 outside players.
4. Only then decide price model; consider launching free + IAP to measure real conversion.
